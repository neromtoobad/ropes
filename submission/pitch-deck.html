02

The loop

One minute, four decisions, real money

Take a seat

10 tUSDC — or free on the house, no wallet, no signature. Deposit once and the bankroll buys every seat after.

Pick a side

UP or DOWN before the window locks. Your whole stack goes in, filled against the live book at whatever price the market gives.

Ride it

The climber tracks BTC against the strike. Bail any second at the live mark — or set an auto-bail and walk away.

The bell

Right side: stack × 1/p, already staked in the next minute. Wrong side: you fall, and lose no more than the seat.

Compounding across windows is the thing the venue structurally cannot offer. A market is neutral and it expires; it will never hold your position into the next one. That gap is the entire game.
